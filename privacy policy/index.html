01

Scope, Applicability & Data Fiduciary

Welcome to Digi Law Reporter (“DLR”, “Platform”, “Application”). This Privacy Policy applies to all services provided across:

  • Web Portal: Accessible at https://digilawreporter.in (and associated subdomains).
  • Mobile Applications: The Digi Law Reporter Android App (distributed on Google Play Store) and iOS App (distributed on Apple App Store).
  • Editorial & Citation Engine: Supreme Court, High Court, and Tribunal judgment databases and headnote indexes.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), Digi Law Reporter operates as the Data Fiduciary, and you are the Data Principal. By installing, browsing, registering, or using our platform, you acknowledge and consent to the data collection and processing practices described herein.

02

Categories of Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of Data Minimization. We collect only what is strictly necessary to authenticate your legal research session and deliver case precedents.

A. Account Registration
  • Full Name: For personalized portal greeting.
  • Mobile Number: Primary login identifier and OTP authentication.
  • Email Address (Optional): For password recovery and invoices.
  • Date of Birth (DOB): For 2-Factor MPIN verification.
  • MPIN: 4-digit PIN (Bcrypt hashed, never readable).
B. Research & Bookmarks
  • Saved Judgments: Bookmarked cases stored in user-scoped PostgreSQL table.
  • Search Queries: Temporary search terms used to generate real-time results.
  • Recent History: Client-side bookmarks persisted in browser local storage.
What We DO NOT Collect: We never collect Government ID documents (Aadhaar, PAN), biometric data, microphone recordings, SMS message contents, contacts list, or GPS location.

04

Zero Disk Storage & PDF Parsing Guarantee

Digi Law Reporter features an innovative Zero-Persistence In-Memory Legal Engine designed specifically to guarantee complete confidentiality for legal practitioners and advocates:

In-Memory RAM Extraction Flow:

Upload PDF -> Node.js Buffer (RAM) -> pdf-parse & Table Extraction -> Output Form Fields -> Immediate Buffer Flush & Garbage Collection

  • Zero Disk Persistence: Uploaded PDF files are never written to hard drives, SSDs, or permanent cloud storage buckets.
  • Zero Client Memory Retention: File inputs reset immediately after parsing.
  • Client-Side Editorial Editing: Extracted judgments exist solely in the active browser DOM canvas until you choose to save or publish.
05

Lawful Grounds for Processing (DPDP Act, 2023)

We process personal data under the following statutory grounds recognized under Section 4 and Section 7 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Consent (Section 6): Obtained explicitly when registering your mobile number and setting up your secure MPIN.
  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver access to searchable judgment volumes, bookmarks, and court headnotes.
  • Legitimate Uses: Security monitoring, prevention of unauthorized scraping, API DDoS mitigation, and system audit logging.
06

Data Security & Cryptographic Standards

We deploy institutional-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect your digital footprint:

Bcrypt Password & MPIN Hashing

All MPINs and admin passwords are salted and hashed (10 rounds). Plaintext passwords never touch our database.

256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption

All data in transit between web/mobile clients and our backend is encrypted using high-grade TLS 1.3 encryption.

JWT & Server-Side Session Tracking

Stateless JSON Web Tokens coupled with active server session tables in PostgreSQL ensure secure expiration and multi-device management.

PostgreSQL Row-Level Access Controls

User bookmarks and saved cases are strictly partitioned and query-scoped to the authenticated user ID.

07

Cookies & Local Storage Policy

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling scripts. We utilize only Strictly Necessary Technical Storage:

  • adminToken / userToken: Stored in browser local storage to maintain your authenticated login session.
  • adminSessionId: Stored to validate single-session active timeouts and auto-lock timers.
  • savedCases: Stored in browser local storage for instant offline bookmark access.
08

Third-Party Disclosures & Cloud Infrastructure

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data.

Data is shared exclusively with certified enterprise infrastructure partners who operate under strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements:

  • Cloud Database & Hosting: Secure PostgreSQL database nodes hosted within Indian jurisdiction data centres.
  • SMS Gateway Providers: To deliver transactional OTP authentication messages.
  • Legal & Statutory Obligations: Only when strictly mandated by a valid court order or statutory directive from Indian law enforcement authorities.
09

Data Retention & Automatic Purge

We retain personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide legal research services.

  • Active Account Data: Retained until you request account deletion.
  • Admin Sessions: Automatically expired and cleared after 1 hour of inactivity.
  • Password Reset Tokens: Automatically expired after 15 minutes and purged upon use.

Privacy & Data Protection FAQs

Does the Digi Law Reporter App track my real-time GPS location?

No. Digi Law Reporter does not require, request, or track your GPS or fine location. All court search parameters are chosen manually by the user.

Are my uploaded PDF case files stored on your servers?

Never. PDF extraction runs 100% in RAM memory. Once judgment text and citations are parsed, the raw PDF buffer is immediately expunged from system memory.

How can I delete my account and all associated bookmarks?

You can delete your account instantly from the app settings menu, or email us at privacy@digilawreporter.in with your registered mobile number for an irreversible database purge.
