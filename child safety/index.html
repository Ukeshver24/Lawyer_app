01

Scope, Purpose & Target Age Group

Digi Law Reporter is a professional digital legal research platform engineered for advocates, judges, law scholars, legal academicians, and law students.

This Child Safety Policy sets forth our institutional safeguards, redaction protocols, and compliance framework under Indian and international child protection laws, specifically governing minors who may access our platform for legal education or whose identities might be referenced in judicial precedents.

02

Statutory Redaction & Masking of Minor Identities

In strict accordance with the mandatory statutory dictates of Indian jurisprudence, Digi Law Reporter enforces automated and editorial pseudonymization protocols for all reported case law:

POCSO Act, 2012 (Section 33(7) & 37)

Names, family details, schools, addresses, or any identifying markers of child victims are permanently replaced with pseudonyms (e.g., “Victim X”, “Child ‘A’”).

Juvenile Justice Act, 2015 (Section 74)

Strict prohibition against disclosing the name, address, or school of any child in conflict with law or child in need of care and protection.

Our editorial index verifies that official Supreme Court and High Court certified transcripts adhere to masking standards before judgment digests and headnotes are published.

03

Section 9 DPDP Act 2023 (Processing of Children’s Data)

Under Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Digi Law Reporter enforces three absolute principles:

  • Zero Behavioral Tracking: We do not track, monitor, or profile the browsing behavior of minors or students accessing our legal index.
  • Zero Targeted Advertising: The platform contains no third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or targeted commercial advertising directed at children.
  • No Harmful Processing: We do not engage in any data processing activity that is likely to cause any detrimental effect on the well-being of a child.
04

Google Play Families Policy & Apple App Store Standards

In alignment with the Google Play Families Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Kids Category & Age Rating):

  • Our mobile applications request only necessary technical network permissions (INTERNET).
  • No background location access, microphone recording, camera recording, or access to sensitive personal storage is initiated without explicit, on-demand user invocation.
  • All API communications utilize encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 connections.
05

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Digi Law Reporter maintains a strict, unconditional Zero Tolerance Policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Mandatory Statutory Reporting:

Any attempted upload or dissemination of unlawful material is immediately blocked by our automated in-memory inspection filters, the offending account is terminated, and the incident is reported to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and relevant statutory law enforcement authorities under Section 67B of the Information Technology Act.

06

Parental & Legal Guardian Rights

Parents and lawful guardians of minors who use Digi Law Reporter for academic, moot court, or educational purposes hold full rights under the DPDP Act 2023 to:

  • Review any student profile or account details registered under their ward's mobile number.
  • Request immediate correction or updating of profile information.
  • Request instantaneous and irreversible deletion of the account and associated search bookmarks.
07

Absence of Predatory Monetization & Loot Mechanisms

Our web and mobile applications do not contain any deceptive design patterns (dark patterns), loot boxes, autoplay paywalls, or behavioral nudges targeting young users. All subscription plans are straightforward, transparent, and intended for adult practitioners and enrolled legal institutions.

Academic & Legal Education Guidelines

When law students or university scholars analyze sensitive constitutional or criminal judgments, they are advised to uphold professional bar ethics and maintain the confidentiality of masked parties in academic moot court memorials, research articles, and symposiums.

Child Safety & Minor Protection FAQs

Are children's names published in criminal or custody judgments?

No. In accordance with POCSO Section 33(7) and JJ Act Section 74, all minor names and sensitive identifying markers are strictly masked and pseudonymized prior to publication.

Does the Digi Law Reporter mobile app track minors or serve ads?

Never. In full compliance with DPDP Act Sec. 9 and Google Play Families Policy, our platform contains zero third-party advertisement networks and conducts zero behavioral profiling.

How can a parent request the deletion of a student account?

Parents or legal guardians can delete the account directly via Settings → Delete Account or email childsafety@digilawreporter.in with the registered mobile number for immediate permanent removal.
