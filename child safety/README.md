## 🏛️ Statutory Compliance Overview

This Child Safety Policy package is specifically engineered to satisfy:
1. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Section 9 - Processing of Personal Data of Children)**:
   - Verifiable parental consent framework for minors.
   - Prohibition against tracking or behavioral monitoring of children.
   - Absolute prohibition against targeted advertising directed at children.
2. **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 (Sections 33(7) & 37)**:
   - Mandatory statutory redaction and pseudonymization of child victim identities in reported case law.
3. **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 (Section 74)**:
   - Statutory prohibition against disclosing any identifying markers of children in conflict with law.
4. **Google Play Families Policy & Developer Program Guidelines**:
   - Age-appropriate design standards.
   - Transparent permissions with zero background access.
   - Absence of predatory monetization or dark patterns.
5. **Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Kids Category & Safety)**:
   - Strict privacy safeguards for student users.
   - Accessible Child Safety Officer contact information.
